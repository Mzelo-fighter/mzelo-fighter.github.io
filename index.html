Goodbye Mzelo

Thanks to the only person who was there for me when I was struggling. I'm not saying their name, but you know who you are. I genuinely appreciate you being there even when you didn't know everything that was going on. Feeling ignored and pushed away when I was already struggling affected me more than most people realized, but you stayed. And that means a lot to me.

To everyone else, I just want to leave one thing with you:

You never really know what someone is dealing with behind the screen. If someone seems off, check in on them. A few kind words or simply listening can make a bigger difference than you might think. Sometimes the smallest act of kindness can mean everything to someone who is struggling.

I've decided to leave Mzelo because coming back here just has not been good for my mental health. I hope this community becomes a place where people look out for each other a little more. I know that might not happen overnight, but I hope this message gives people something to think about.

Goodbye, and I wish everyone nothing but the best.
